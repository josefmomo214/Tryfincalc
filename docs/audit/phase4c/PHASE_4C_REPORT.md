### Homebuyer playbook

1. set a personal budget;
2. compare written loan terms on the same basis;
3. inspect the property and price material findings;
4. review offer and contract terms together;
5. match a written rate lock to the closing schedule;
6. verify cash-to-close, final figures, wire instructions, walkthrough obligations, and first-payment timing.

The merged guide removes duplicated steps, market-timing claims, and false freshness tied to the year. No publication or modification date was invented.
